How WinSports Works · Fair Play Rules

WinSports is a 100% free-to-play sports prediction platform. Make predictions on real matches, score points based on your results, and compete on the leaderboard.

✓ No Entry Fees
✓ No Deposits
🚫 No Betting
🛡️ 100% Free Game

1. How Prediction Points Work

  • Each match shows the points you can win or lose for each pick before you make your choice.
  • Once you submit a prediction, its point values are locked in.
  • When the official result is verified, points are awarded or deducted based on whether your prediction was correct.
  • Prediction points come only from your prediction results. They cannot be bought, and they cannot be earned through referrals or advertisements.

2. Monthly Leaderboard

  • Players are ranked by the prediction points they earn from matches played during the month. Separate monthly and all-time leaderboards are shown.
  • A minimum of 200 picks (predictions) is required to be eligible for a medal.
  • The top 3 eligible players may receive the announced monthly gift-voucher prizes, subject to residence (India only), age, state and other eligibility rules in our Terms & Conditions.
  • Leaderboard position is determined by prediction performance only. Referral credits do not contribute to leaderboard points.

3. Referral Credits

  • WinSports also has a separate referral-credit system. Share your referral link from My Account; once a friend registers with it and makes their first prediction, you earn referral credits.
  • Referral credits are completely separate from prediction points. They do not add prediction points, do not affect leaderboard rankings, and cannot be converted into prediction points.
  • Once you reach the required number of credits, you can redeem them separately for an eligible gift voucher from My Account (available to residents of India only), according to the referral and redemption rules in our Terms & Conditions.

4. Two Separate Paths

🎮 Predictions
Prediction Points → Leaderboard → Monthly Top 3 Prizes
🤝 Referrals
Referral Activity → Referral Credits → Separate Voucher Redemption

These two paths are independent: activity in one never changes the other.

5. Advertisements

  • WinSports may show third-party advertisements to keep the platform free.
  • Viewing or clicking an advertisement never earns prediction points, referral credits, vouchers or any other advantage.
See also our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. Questions? Contact the admin at winsportsapp@gmail.com.
