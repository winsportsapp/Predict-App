Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we may collect your username, email address, and profile details.
  • Google Sign-In: If you sign up or log in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with us. We use your name only to suggest your username, and never see your Google password.
  • Verification Data: If you win a prize, we may request your state of residence and age verification details to comply with regional Indian laws.
  • Referral Data: If you join using a referral link or refer others, we record the referral, your referral credit balance, and your voucher redemption history.
  • Match Alerts: If you turn on match alerts, we store a notification address and keys for your browser or device, provided by its push service (such as Google, Apple or Mozilla), so we can send you alerts.
  • Usage Data: We collect data regarding your predictions, leaderboard scores, and interactions with the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To maintain your account, track leaderboard points, and rank users.
  • To verify your eligibility and distribute gift vouchers to the top 3 winners.
  • To credit referrals and process voucher redemptions.
  • To send match alerts you have turned on. You can turn them off at any time in your browser or device settings.
  • To display advertisements, which may be personalized by third-party advertising providers.

3. Third-Party Advertisements & Cookies

  • We may use third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements on our Website. These providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information (such as your IP address or browsing behavior) for advertising and measurement.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We may share data only if required by Indian law enforcement authorities or to comply with statutory legal processes.

5. Contact Us

For any questions regarding these Terms or Privacy Policy, please contact the admin at: winsportsapp@gmail.com.
