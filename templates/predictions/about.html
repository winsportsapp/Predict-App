About WinSports

WinSports (winsports.cc) is a free-to-play sports prediction platform for sports fans across India. You predict the outcomes of real matches, earn points based on your analytical accuracy, and compete with other fans on monthly and all-time leaderboards.

What You Can Predict

We publish daily fixtures across Football, Cricket, Tennis, Badminton, and Hockey. Each match shows the points you can win or lose for each pick before you choose, so you always know how each selection can affect your score.

100% Free to Play

There are no entry fees, no deposits, and no betting or wagering of any kind. Nothing is required to play or to be eligible for prizes. Each month, the top 3 eligible players on the leaderboard may receive gift vouchers. Prizes are available to residents of India only. See our Terms & Conditions.

Fair Play

Leaderboard rankings are based only on prediction points. Referral credits, advertisements, and anything else outside your predictions never change your score. Multiple accounts, bots, and other attempts to manipulate the leaderboard are strictly prohibited. Read the full rules on our How It Works page.

Sports Analysis & Methodology

In addition to our daily prediction game, WinSports evaluates match contests using structured sports analysis. Our analytical methodology is centered on objective, observable match variables:

  • Pitch & Surface Conditions: Analyzing soil composition (red vs. black clay), grass cover, and bounce characteristics in cricket, or court pace index (CPI) in tennis.
  • Venue & Environmental Factors: Assessing boundary geometry, altitude, prevailing cross-winds, humidity, and evening dew dynamics.
  • Tactical Structures: Evaluating formation dynamics, pressing traps, transition speeds, and defensive block depths in football.
  • Phase Specialization: Examining powerplay aggression, middle-overs strike rotation, and death-over bowling execution in T20 cricket.
  • Contextual Head-to-Head Analysis: Reviewing recent tactical encounters while filtering out outdated historical records that featured obsolete rosters or coaching philosophies.

Automated Sports Data vs. Analytical Guidelines

To ensure absolute clarity and transparency for our visitors:

  • Automated Fixture & Scoring Data: Match schedules, kickoff timestamps, team rosters, live scores, and verified final outcomes displayed on our match cards are synchronized automatically through third-party sports data feeds (FlashLive).
  • Independent Analytical Guidelines: Educational guidelines and match previews are formulated independently by the WinSports team. Read our full collection of tactical and rules guides in our Sports Insights section. Analytical content is strictly informational; it never alters match points, locked odds, or leaderboard scoring.
